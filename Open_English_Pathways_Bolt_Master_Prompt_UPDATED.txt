RESOURCE GOVERNANCE — CRITICAL
- The audit workbook is a research/audit source, not a blanket approval to publish every row.
- Publish as approved core resources only rows explicitly approved for that level and whose status is sufficiently verified.
- HOLD, PARTIAL, NOT VERIFIABLE, SUPPLEMENT ONLY, STRETCH ONLY, mixed-level, and pending rows must be hidden from default core recommendations or visibly marked “Under review”, “Supplement”, or “Not CEFR-certified”.
- Never convert a provider's course numbering into a CEFR level without evidence.
- Mixed-level series (e.g., A1/A2 or B1/B2 or B2/C1) must be classified at exact item/episode level only when evidence supports it.
- TOEFL is independent of CEFR and must remain in its own preparation section.
- Free access does not equal permission to reuse. Link to original publisher pages. Do not download, copy, mirror, rehost, scrape, or reproduce copyrighted books, videos, PDFs, audio, images, transcripts, or exercises without permission.
- Preserve publisher names and exact URLs. Open external resources in a new tab with safe rel attributes and label them as external links.
- Add “Last reviewed” and “Review status” fields where present. If dates are missing, do not invent them.
- Add an admin review queue for all unresolved records.
